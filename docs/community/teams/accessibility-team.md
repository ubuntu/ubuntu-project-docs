# Accessibility team

The Ubuntu Accessibility team works to raise the level of accessibility support within Ubuntu and its derivatives.
Accessibility refers to software used primarily by people who are deaf, are mobility impaired, or have sight problems including color blindness or dyslexia.


## Communication

**Mailing list**
: [ubuntu-accessibility](https://lists.ubuntu.com/mailman/listinfo/ubuntu-accessibility)

**IRC**
: `#ubuntu-accessibility` on [Libera.Chat](https://libera.chat/)

**Launchpad**
: [~accessibility](https://launchpad.net/~accessibility)


## Meetings

The team typically holds monthly meetings on IRC in `#ubuntu-accessibility`.
Meeting times and topics are announced on the ubuntu-accessibility mailing list.


## Authority

The Accessibility team is an open community team; anyone interested in improving accessibility in Ubuntu can join through the [~accessibility](https://launchpad.net/~accessibility) team on Launchpad.


## Contributing

Ways to help include testing accessibility features, identifying software the team would like to see improved, and helping with planning and documentation.
[Sign up on Launchpad](https://launchpad.net/~accessibility/+join) to join the team, and introduce yourself on the mailing list or IRC channel.
