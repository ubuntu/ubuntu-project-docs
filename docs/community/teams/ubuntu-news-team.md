# Ubuntu News team

The Ubuntu News team exists to gather and publish news on Ubuntu-related stories, all in accordance with the {ref}`Ubuntu Code of Conduct <code-of-conduct>`.


## Communication

**IRC**
: `#ubuntu-news` on [Libera.Chat](https://libera.chat/)

**Mailing list**
: [ubuntu-news-team](https://lists.ubuntu.com/mailman/listinfo/ubuntu-news-team)

**Launchpad**
: [~ubuntu-news](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-news)


## Meetings

No regular public meeting is currently advertised.


## Authority

The News team is an open community team.
There is no core team membership requirement: any contribution is appreciated and you don't have to make a significant investment of time to participate.


## Contributing

Getting involved is as simple as joining the team and exploring a little.
You can help gather stories, write summaries, and publish through the team's news outlets:

**The Fridge**
: [The Fridge](https://fridge.ubuntu.com/) is an information hub for the Ubuntu community, bringing together news, grassroots marketing, advocacy, team collaboration, and original content about Ubuntu.

**Ubuntu Weekly Newsletter**
: The [Ubuntu Weekly Newsletter](https://discourse.ubuntu.com/c/uwn/124) collects Ubuntu news from around the community and around the world to bring readers a weekly dose of Ubuntu articles.

**Developer news**
: The team also aims to get weekly news out about Ubuntu development.

Join the `#ubuntu-news` channel or the ubuntu-news-team mailing list to find out what is currently being worked on.
