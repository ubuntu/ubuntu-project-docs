# Lubuntu team

Lubuntu is an official flavor of Ubuntu that provides a lightweight and easy-to-use distribution based on Ubuntu and {spellexception}`LXQt`.
The Lubuntu team's home is [lubuntu.me](https://lubuntu.me/), and its project resources live on [Launchpad](https://launchpad.net/lubuntu) and the [Lubuntu wiki](https://git.lubuntu.me/lubuntu-wiki/wiki/wiki).


## Communication

**Matrix**
: The {matrix}`lubuntu` space brings together the team's rooms, including {matrix}`lubuntu-support`, {matrix}`lubuntu-devel`, {matrix}`lubuntu-discuss`, and the read-only {matrix}`lubuntu-news`

**Mailing lists**
: [lubuntu-users](https://lists.ubuntu.com/mailman/listinfo/lubuntu-users) for support and [lubuntu-devel](https://lists.ubuntu.com/mailman/listinfo/lubuntu-devel) for development

**Discourse**
: The [Lubuntu section](https://discourse.ubuntu.com/c/flavors/lubuntu/343) of the Ubuntu Discourse

**Telegram**
: [Lubuntu announcements](https://t.me/LubuntuOfficial), with community channels in several languages linked from the [Lubuntu links page](https://lubuntu.me/links/)


## Meetings

No regular public meeting is currently advertised.
Development coordination happens in the {matrix}`lubuntu-devel` room and on the lubuntu-devel mailing list.


## Authority

Lubuntu development is led by the project's release manager together with its long-standing contributors.
Sustained contributors can become Lubuntu Members; see the [contributing appendix](https://manual.lubuntu.me/master/B/Contributing.html) of the Lubuntu Manual for details.


## Contributing

Lubuntu relies on the contributions of volunteers, and you do not need to be a developer to help.
The [contributing appendix](https://manual.lubuntu.me/master/B/Contributing.html) of the Lubuntu Manual describes the main areas:

* **Support and bug triage**: help users solve their problems and turn confirmed issues into actionable bug reports (see the [bug reporting help](https://git.lubuntu.me/lubuntu-wiki/wiki/wiki/Bugs))
* **Documentation**: help maintain the [Lubuntu Manual](https://manual.lubuntu.me/) and wiki
* **Testing**: test development releases and report bugs

New contributors are welcome in the {matrix}`lubuntu-devel` room, where the team is happy to provide mentorship for your first tasks.
