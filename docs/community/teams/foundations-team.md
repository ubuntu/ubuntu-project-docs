# Foundations team

The Ubuntu Foundations team (formerly Platform) is responsible for delivering the core Ubuntu system, which is common to the whole Ubuntu family of products and services.
This core of Ubuntu is the rock upon which all other Ubuntu technology is based, and is a usable, high-quality operating system in its own right for users around the world.
It strives to be the best free software development platform in existence.

The core system is built from many component projects, most of which are built by free software developers around the world.
The team selects the best available packages for the job, and molds them into a clean, free, well-integrated system, making changes where required to meet Ubuntu's high standards of quality and ease of use.
It works with developers in the free software community to support their interests as well as Ubuntu's.

Specific areas that the Foundations team is responsible for include, in no particular order:

* Maintenance of central package infrastructure such as the Ubuntu archive and seeds
* Release engineering and management
* Development standards and collaborative development support
* The bulk of merging changes from Debian at the start of each release cycle
* The toolchain (compiler, C library, and so on)
* Package management
* Crash reporting and the Ubuntu Error Tracker
* The installers and installation images

## Meetings

No regular public meeting is currently advertised.


## Authority

The Foundations team is a Canonical engineering team.
Its current membership is best viewed through its associated [Launchpad group](https://launchpad.net/~canonical-foundations).

The team is responsible for how the various pieces of the larger system fit together, and holds upload rights for the core system packages in the Ubuntu archive.
