# QA team

The Ubuntu Quality (QA) team develops and maintains the policies and practices for ensuring Ubuntu's quality as a distribution, and provides general advice, oversight, and leadership of the QA community and its activities within the Ubuntu project.


## Communication

**Mailing list**
: [ubuntu-quality](https://lists.ubuntu.com/mailman/listinfo/ubuntu-quality). Announcements and coordination for testing occur on this list.

**IRC**
: `#ubuntu-quality` on [Libera.Chat](https://libera.chat/)

**Launchpad**
: [~ubuntu-testing](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-testing) is open for everybody helping out the QA team to join


## Meetings

No regular public meeting is currently advertised.
Testing milestones and calls for testing are announced on the ubuntu-quality mailing list.


## Authority

The QA team provides advice, oversight, and leadership for quality assurance activities within the Ubuntu project.
It maintains the policies and practices used to ensure Ubuntu's quality as a distribution.


## Contributing

You can get involved with the QA team in several ways; see {ref}`contribute-to-qa-and-testing` for an overview of the roles you can take on.

Once you have found the areas where you want to help:

1. Subscribe to the [ubuntu-quality mailing list](https://lists.ubuntu.com/mailman/listinfo/ubuntu-quality); announcements and coordination for testing occur there.
2. Join the [~ubuntu-testing](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-testing) team on Launchpad.
3. Stop by `#ubuntu-quality` on [Libera.Chat](https://libera.chat/) and say hello; you can ask for guidance on getting started both on the mailing list and on IRC.
