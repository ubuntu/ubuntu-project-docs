# Ubuntu Kylin team

Ubuntu Kylin is an official flavor of Ubuntu that provides an operating system catering to the specific needs of Chinese users.
The objective of the Ubuntu Kylin project is to create a variant of Ubuntu that is more suitable for Chinese users, providing a delicate, thoughtful, and fully customized Chinese user experience out of the box — for instance, through a desktop user interface localized in Chinese and default software commonly used in China.

Ubuntu Kylin has been a formal member of the Ubuntu family since Ubuntu Kylin 13.04.

## Authority

The Ubuntu Kylin team is organized into the Launchpad teams listed above, covering development, quality assurance, release, community, and documentation.
The flavor lead and contacts are listed through the team's Launchpad membership.


## Contributing

The Ubuntu Kylin community works on various aspects of the distribution, providing advice and technical support, and helping to promote Ubuntu Kylin to a wider audience.
You can:

* Get the [source code](https://code.launchpad.net/~ubuntukylin-members) hosted on Launchpad and start hacking
* [Report bugs to Ubuntu Kylin](https://bugs.launchpad.net/ubuntukylin) or browse existing bugs (see {ref}`how-to-report-a-bug` for how to write a good bug report)
* Contact the team through the ubuntukylin-devel mailing list to find out where your help is most needed
