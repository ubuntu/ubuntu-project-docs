# Kubuntu team

Kubuntu is an official flavor of Ubuntu that provides the KDE desktop and software, integrating KDE with Ubuntu.
The Kubuntu team aims to provide a robust, high-quality desktop computing experience based on KDE Plasma and the broader KDE software ecosystem.

The team's contributor resources are maintained on the [Kubuntu section of the KDE community wiki](https://community.kde.org/Kubuntu).


## Communication

## Meetings

Team meetings are announced on the [Kubuntu meeting page](https://community.kde.org/Kubuntu/Meeting) on the KDE community wiki, which also hosts details of the annual community meeting.


## Authority

Kubuntu is governed by the Kubuntu Council; council elections, membership, and project policies are documented on the [Kubuntu section of the KDE community wiki](https://community.kde.org/Kubuntu).
Sustained contributors can apply to become Kubuntu members.


## Contributing

Whether you want to report a bug, test a new release, package software, or just get involved, there is a place for you:

* **Testing**: try out pre-release software and report what breaks. ISO testing is coordinated through the [Ubuntu ISO Tracker](https://iso.qa.ubuntu.com/).
* **Bug triage**: help work through incoming bug reports; see the [Kubuntu bug triage guide](https://community.kde.org/Kubuntu/BugTriage).
* **Packaging**: help maintain Kubuntu's packages; see the [Kubuntu packaging guide](https://community.kde.org/Kubuntu/Packaging). The team is available in {matrix}`kubuntu-devel` and on the kubuntu-devel mailing list to help you get started.

Security issues in Kubuntu packages should be reported privately to the {ref}`security team <security-team>`; for issues in upstream KDE software, contact [KDE Security](https://kde.org/info/security/).
