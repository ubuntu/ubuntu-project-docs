# Ubuntu universe packaging team

The Ubuntu universe packaging team, better known as the Masters of the Universe (MOTU), keeps the `universe` and `multiverse` components of Ubuntu in shape.
MOTUs are community members who spend their time adding, maintaining, and supporting as much as possible of the software found in `universe`.
A list of MOTUs can be found on the [~motu](https://launchpad.net/~motu) team page on Launchpad.

Besides the MOTUs, there is also a large number of volunteers called contributors who are on their way to becoming MOTUs, and who have their work on `universe` sponsored by MOTUs.

## Authority

MOTUs are Ubuntu developers with upload rights for the `universe` and `multiverse` components of the Ubuntu archive.
Contributors who do not have upload rights yet work by using MOTUs as sponsors for their work.


## Contributing

There are no requirements or exams to pass to become a MOTU contributor — just a willingness to learn and a commitment to the {ref}`Ubuntu Code of Conduct <code-of-conduct>`.

* {ref}`new-packages` explains how to get a brand new package into Ubuntu
* {ref}`sponsorship` and {ref}`how-to-find-a-sponsor` explain how to get your changes reviewed and uploaded
* {ref}`ubuntu-developer-membership` explains what it takes to become an Ubuntu developer with upload rights

The team primarily works on merges and syncs from Debian, and on fixing bugs.
Current work items and processes are discussed on the ubuntu-motu mailing list and the `#ubuntu-motu` IRC channel.
