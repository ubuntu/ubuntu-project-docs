# Wiki team

The Wiki team is a group of active wiki contributors working together to maintain and improve the Ubuntu wiki.
The current home of the Ubuntu wiki is [wiki.ubuntu.com](https://wiki.ubuntu.com/).


## Communication

**Mailing list**
: [ubuntu-doc](https://lists.ubuntu.com/mailman/listinfo/ubuntu-doc), where the wiki is discussed together with the rest of Ubuntu's documentation


## Meetings

No regular public meeting is currently advertised.


## Authority

The Wiki team operates as part of the {ref}`documentation team <documentation-team>`.
The wiki is an open, community-driven resource: anyone with an account can edit most pages, while wiki administrators look after the site itself.


## Contributing

The ways to help the wiki are almost limitless.
Here are a few things you can start with:

* Create an account on [wiki.ubuntu.com](https://wiki.ubuntu.com/) and improve the pages you use
* Help migrate content from the archived wikis in the [ubuntu/wiki-archives](https://github.com/ubuntu/wiki-archives) repository to the current wiki
* Proofread pages: follow the instructions, click the links, and check spelling and grammar

Discuss the wiki on the [ubuntu-doc mailing list](https://lists.ubuntu.com/mailman/listinfo/ubuntu-doc), and volunteer to help there too.
