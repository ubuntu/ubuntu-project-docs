# IRC team

The Ubuntu IRC team is responsible for keeping the main Ubuntu and derivatives Internet Relay Chat (IRC) channels a nice place to be in.
The Ubuntu IRC channels live on the [Libera.Chat](https://libera.chat/) network.

Some core channels are directly managed by the IRC team, although there are many other official channels that are managed by teams more closely related to each channel's scope.
All official channels are coordinated with the help of the IRC Council, which also acts as the governance body for the IRC team itself.

On channels that fall under the scope of the IRC team, everyone is asked to adhere to the user guidelines, while channel operators follow the operator guidelines, in the spirit of the {ref}`Ubuntu Code of Conduct <code-of-conduct>`.
If you feel you have been treated unjustly by the IRC team, you can use the team's dispute resolution process to settle the issue.


## Communication

**IRC**
: `#ubuntu-irc` is the channel all Ubuntu operators are invited to use for inter-channel communication, and where the team is reachable by everyone in real time. `#ubuntu-ops` is dedicated to emergency communication and to solving disputes between users and operators of channels directly managed by the IRC team.

**Mailing lists**
: The [ubuntu-irc](https://lists.ubuntu.com/mailman/listinfo/ubuntu-irc) list is used by the team for proposals and information of interest to all Ubuntu IRC operators. The [irc-council](https://lists.ubuntu.com/mailman/listinfo/irc-council) address is available for email inquiries, also when confidentiality is desired.

**Launchpad**
: [~ubuntu-irc](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-irc) lists the team members, and [~ubuntu-irc-council](https://launchpad.net/~ubuntu-irc-council) lists the IRC Council members


## Meetings

The IRC Council holds meetings in `#ubuntu-meeting` when important issues need to be discussed.
Logs of the meetings are available from [irclogs.ubuntu.com](https://irclogs.ubuntu.com/).


## Authority

The IRC Council acts as the governance body of the IRC team and as the reference point for channel coordination across all official Ubuntu IRC channels.
Members of the IRC team are trusted operators on many of the core Ubuntu channels, and form a pool that other channels can draw on when they need more operators.


## Contributing

General Ubuntu support on IRC is offered on a voluntary basis by everyone in `#ubuntu`; simply helping other users in the channels is already a valuable contribution.

You can contact the IRC team in real time in `#ubuntu-irc` to report channel problems, or for other inquiries best dealt with interactively.
For serious channel emergencies that require immediate attention (and for such issues only), you can say `!ops` in the channel to alert the operators.

To enhance cohesion among the Ubuntu IRC community, the team offers hostname cloaks to {ref}`Ubuntu Members <ubuntu-membership>` who request them.
The team also runs {spellexception}`ubottu`, an expert system ("{spellexception}`infobot`") that provides useful information about Ubuntu in the channels, and that you can also contribute to.
